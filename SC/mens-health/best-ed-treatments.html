Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most effective ED treatment?
Tadalafil and sildenafil are equally effective for most men, with 70-85% success rates. Tadalafil lasts up to 36 hours while sildenafil lasts 4-6 hours. The best option depends on individual lifestyle, medical history, and personal preference.
Can I get ED medication prescribed online in South Carolina?
Yes, South Carolina permits licensed physicians to prescribe ED medications via telehealth after proper medical evaluation. No in-person visit required. Consultations are confidential and HIPAA-compliant.
How much does ED treatment cost in South Carolina?
Generic tadalafil costs $30-$90/month, generic sildenafil costs $20-$60/month depending on dosage and frequency. SouthCarolinaDoc offers transparent pricing with physician consultation included.
Is generic Cialis as effective as brand Cialis?
Yes, generic tadalafil contains the same active ingredient and is bioequivalent to brand Cialis. It works identically but costs 90% less. The FDA requires generic medications to meet the same quality and effectiveness standards.
Are there side effects from ED medications?
Common side effects are mild and include headache, flushing, nasal congestion, and upset stomach. These occur in 10-15% of users and are typically temporary. Serious side effects are rare but require immediate medical attention.
What's the difference between daily and as-needed tadalafil?
Daily tadalafil (2.5-5mg) provides continuous coverage for spontaneous intimacy, while as-needed (10-20mg) is taken before anticipated activity. Daily dosing also improves urinary symptoms and may benefit overall vascular health.
