OhioDoc – Online Doctor Visits for Ohio Residents

OhioDoc is Ohio's dedicated telemedicine platform connecting residents with licensed, board-certified physicians — all from the comfort of home. Whether you need a same-day virtual consultation, an online prescription refill, or expert guidance on a new health concern, OhioDoc makes quality healthcare accessible, affordable, and convenient. No long waits, no unnecessary office visits — just real medical care when and where you need it.

What Is OhioDoc?

OhioDoc is a state-licensed telemedicine service built specifically for Ohio residents. The platform allows patients to connect with licensed Ohio physicians through secure video visits, phone consultations, and online messaging — all without leaving home.

Founded to address the growing need for accessible healthcare across Ohio's urban centers and rural communities alike, OhioDoc serves patients in Columbus, Cleveland, Cincinnati, Toledo, Akron, and every ZIP code in between.

How OhioDoc Works

Getting care through OhioDoc is straightforward:

  1. Create your account – Sign up in minutes with basic health and insurance information.
  2. Choose your visit type – Select from video, phone, or asynchronous (messaging) consultations.
  3. See a licensed Ohio doctor – Connect with a board-certified physician who is licensed to practice in Ohio.
  4. Receive your care plan – Get diagnoses, treatment recommendations, and prescriptions sent directly to your preferred Ohio pharmacy.

What Conditions Can OhioDoc Treat?

OhioDoc physicians are equipped to evaluate, diagnose, and treat a wide range of common medical conditions. Telemedicine is appropriate for many — though not all — health concerns.

Common Conditions Treated Online

  • Respiratory infections – Colds, flu, sinus infections, bronchitis, and COVID-19 evaluation
  • Urinary tract infections (UTIs) – Diagnosis and antibiotic prescriptions for uncomplicated UTIs
  • Skin conditions – Rashes, acne, eczema, allergic reactions, and minor skin infections
  • Mental health – Anxiety, depression, stress management, and medication management
  • Chronic disease management – Ongoing care for diabetes, hypertension, high cholesterol, and thyroid conditions
  • Allergies – Seasonal allergies, allergic rhinitis, and medication adjustments
  • Women's health – Birth control consultations, UTI treatment, and general gynecological concerns
  • Men's health – Erectile dysfunction, hair loss, and preventive care
  • Weight management – Medical weight loss consultations, GLP-1 medications, and lifestyle guidance
  • Prescription refills – Continuity of care for existing medications

When to Seek Emergency Care Instead

OhioDoc is not a substitute for emergency medical services. Please call 911 or visit your nearest emergency room if you experience:

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
  • Signs of stroke (facial drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty)
  • Severe abdominal pain
  • Loss of consciousness
  • Serious injuries or trauma

Frequently Asked Questions About OhioDoc

Is OhioDoc legitimate and licensed?

Yes. OhioDoc operates fully within Ohio state law and telehealth regulations. All physicians on the platform hold active Ohio medical licenses and are board-certified in their respective specialties.

Can OhioDoc prescribe medications?

Yes. OhioDoc physicians can prescribe a wide range of medications, including antibiotics, antihistamines, blood pressure medications, antidepressants, and more. Controlled substances are subject to federal and Ohio-specific regulations and may require additional steps.

Does OhioDoc accept my insurance?

OhioDoc works with many major insurance providers in Ohio. You can verify your coverage during the sign-up process. Affordable self-pay rates are also available for uninsured patients.

How quickly can I see a doctor on OhioDoc?

Most patients connect with a physician within minutes to a few hours, depending on the time of day and visit type selected. Same-day appointments are typically available seven days a week.

Is my information safe with OhioDoc?

Absolutely. OhioDoc uses a HIPAA-compliant, encrypted platform to protect all patient health information. Your data is never sold or shared without your explicit consent.

What if I need a specialist?

If your condition requires specialist care, your OhioDoc physician will provide a referral and help coordinate next steps. OhioDoc also connects patients with Ohio-based specialists for select telehealth-appropriate specialty consultations.
