When Should You See an OH Doc Online vs. In Person?

Knowing when telemedicine is the right choice is important for your health and safety. Here's a simple guide:

Online Telemedicine Is Right For You When:

  • Your symptoms are mild to moderate and not life-threatening
  • You need a prescription refill for a stable, existing condition
  • You're experiencing symptoms you've had before and know how to describe
  • You want a second opinion or have a general health question
  • You live in a rural area of Ohio with limited access to nearby clinics
  • You have a busy schedule and cannot take time off work for an in-person visit
  • You want to avoid exposure to other sick patients in a waiting room

Go to an Emergency Room or Urgent Care If You Experience:

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
  • Signs of stroke (facial drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty)
  • Severe abdominal pain
  • High fever with stiff neck or confusion
  • Serious injuries or wounds
  • Allergic reactions with throat swelling

Your health and safety always come first. OhioDoc physicians are trained to recognize when a situation exceeds what telemedicine can safely address and will direct you accordingly.
