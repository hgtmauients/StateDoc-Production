Tadalafil vs Sildenafil: Complete ED Medication Comparison 2026

Tadalafil and sildenafil are both PDE5 inhibitors used for erectile dysfunction. Neither one is a hormone, and neither one creates an erection without sexual stimulation. The useful difference is how long the effect lasts and whether the medicine is taken as needed or every day.

Duration

Sildenafil is the as-needed drug with the shorter window. People take it before sexual activity, and the effect is measured in hours, not in a day. Tadalafil is the longer-acting drug. Its effect can last up to 36 hours, which is why it is the one people choose when they do not want to time a dose to the hour.

A longer window is not a stronger drug. A person who does not respond to one PDE5 inhibitor sometimes responds to the other, and sometimes responds to a different dose of the same one. That is a follow-up decision. It is not a reason to take both on the same day.

As needed or every day

Sildenafil is used as needed. Tadalafil can be prescribed as needed or as a smaller daily dose. The daily dose is for people who want the medicine on board without planning each time, and it is also the form used when the clinician is treating urinary symptoms from an enlarged prostate at the same time. A daily dose is a different prescription from an as-needed dose. Taking the as-needed tablet every day, because it seemed to work, is not the daily regimen.

Timing and food

Sildenafil is affected by a heavy, high-fat meal, which can delay it. Tadalafil is not tied to meals in the same way. Alcohol in quantity works against both, because it lowers the chance of an erection and adds to dizziness. Grapefruit is a reason to ask the clinician, not a home rule to invent. The dose itself, for either drug, is the one written on that prescription. Tablets are not split or stacked to chase a faster result.

Who should not take them

Anyone taking a nitrate for chest pain, or using a recreational nitrate, should not take either drug. The combination can drop blood pressure to a dangerous level. Riociguat is another drug that does not mix with this class. Alpha blockers for prostate symptoms or blood pressure can add to dizziness, and the clinician has to know about them before the first dose.

Chest pain, fainting, or an erection that has not gone down within four hours is an emergency, not a side effect to wait out. Sudden loss of vision or hearing is a reason to stop the drug and get care. These warnings are the same for a telehealth prescription and an in-person one. How the choice gets made

The clinician asks how often the problem happens, whether erections are present in the morning or with masturbation, what heart disease or blood pressure treatment is already in place, and what the person wants the timing to look like. Tadalafil fits when a long window or a daily dose matches that answer. Sildenafil fits when a shorter, as-needed dose is enough and the cost or the prior response favors it. Neither drug is appropriate when the history points to a cardiac emergency, a hormone problem that has not been evaluated, or a relationship to nitrates.

Online offers that ship either drug without that history are not a version of this comparison. They skip the step the comparison depends on.

What this article does not do

This page explains the decision. It does not approve a medication, set a dose, or replace a clinician who can see the rest of the history. Seek in-person or emergency care for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms.
