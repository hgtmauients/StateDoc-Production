Is Medical Weight Loss Right for You?

Many people struggle with weight despite trying diet and exercise on their own. If you've found it difficult to lose weight or keep it off, you're not alone — and there may be underlying medical factors at play. A physician-supervised approach to weight loss looks at the full picture of your health, including hormones, metabolism, lifestyle, and medical history.

Signs You May Benefit from Medical Weight Loss Support

  • You've tried dieting or exercising without lasting results
  • Your BMI is 27 or higher, especially with a related health condition
  • You have conditions like type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, or sleep apnea linked to your weight
  • You experience intense food cravings or difficulty feeling full
  • You feel fatigued, sluggish, or notice unexplained weight gain
  • You're interested in FDA-approved weight loss medications

Prescription Weight Loss Medications

For eligible patients, prescription medications can be a powerful tool alongside lifestyle changes. Commonly discussed options include:

  • GLP-1 receptor agonists (such as semaglutide or tirzepatide) — medications that help regulate appetite and blood sugar
  • Appetite suppressants — short-term options to help reduce hunger during early weight loss
  • Metabolic support medications — tailored to your individual health profile

Your physician will review your medical history, current medications, and health goals before recommending any treatment.

Lifestyle and Behavioral Guidance

Medication works best alongside sustainable lifestyle changes. Your LouisianaDoc provider can offer guidance on:

  • Nutrition strategies that support long-term weight management
  • Physical activity recommendations based on your current health
  • Behavioral approaches to reduce emotional or stress-related eating

When to See a Doctor for Weight Loss

You should consider speaking with a physician about your weight if:

  • Your weight is affecting your quality of life, mobility, or mental health
  • You have a weight-related medical condition that isn't improving
  • Over-the-counter supplements or fad diets haven't produced safe, lasting results
  • You want a medically guided plan with professional oversight

Early intervention can prevent weight-related conditions from worsening. Don't wait until a health crisis to ask for help.
